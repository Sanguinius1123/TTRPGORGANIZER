# Premade Characters — Foxtrot Cluster Open Table

Seven premade characters, one per S&V playbook. Players pick one at the start of a session — no prior knowledge required. Each is a freelancer without a ship, operating out of Songah on Sonhandra.

> **GM note on gear:** Players have access to ALL items listed on their playbook sheet. At the start of each session/score, they choose Load (Light 3 / Normal 5 / Heavy 6) and check off what they're carrying. No purchasing required. The "Notable items" below are the ones worth calling out; everything else on the standard item list is also available.

---

## 1 — MECHANIC: "Cog" Vera Kass

**Species:** Chelkin (Tropical coloring — warm rust-brown fur, six limbs)  
**Heritage:** Sohan Industrial District  
**Background:** Labor  
**Vice:** Work (can't stop tinkering; stress goes down when she's fixing something)

**The short version:** Cog maintained Guild mining and maglev equipment in the Sohan Industrial District for eight years. When she filed a formal safety report on a cracked pressure coupling that the Guild was covering up, her contract was voided within a week. Now she freelances — keeps machines alive for whoever will pay — and she carries a deep, professional hatred of the Guild of Engineers.

**Action Ratings**
- Rig 3, Hack 2, Study 2, Scramble 1

**Starting Special Ability:** Tinker  
**Second Ability (choose one):** Overclock or Pack Rat

**Default Load:** Normal (5)  
**Notable items:** Fine Hacking Rig, Small Drone, Vision Enhancing Goggles, Repair Tools

**Contacts in Songah**
- *Labor Compact Hall* — the rep there knows her from Sohan. Good source of work and information about industrial operations in the District.
- *The Stitch* — Mira patched her up after a gantry collapse three years ago. Trusts Mira unconditionally.

**Personality hook:** Talks about machinery the way other people talk about animals — it's alive, it has preferences, it tells you things. She's also small enough to fit inside most of it, which she considers a professional advantage.

## 2 — MUSCLE: Sauk Thren

**Species:** Keth'vari (Military/Labor caste — copper scales, 8ft, heavy build)  
**Heritage:** Maelstrom  
**Background:** Military  
**Vice:** Pleasure (fighting — not the violence specifically, the clarity of it)

**The short version:** Sauk was an Ashen Knives debt enforcer for four years — reliable, professional, efficient. That ended when a Knives lieutenant named Orev tried to use Sauk as a patsy on a job that went wrong. Sauk killed Orev instead. He's been a problem for the Knives since then and they haven't let it go. He mostly works for people who have Knives problems of their own, because at least that's a shared interest.

**Action Ratings**
- Scrap 3, Command 2, Scramble 1, Skulk 1

**Starting Special Ability:** Unstoppable  
**Second Ability (choose one):** Not to be Trifled With or Veteran

**Signature Weapon:** Sunder (vibro-blade — named, carried always, counts as 2 load)  
**Default Load:** Heavy (6)  
**Notable items:** Sunder, Armor, Heavy Blaster

**Contacts in Songah**
- *Del Hex's Fighting Ring* — Del Hex knows him. Professional respect. He's done pit fights there between jobs; she's neutral on his Knives situation.
- *The Offload* — regular customer. They know him as a buyer, not a talker.

**Personality hook:** Does not volunteer information. Answers direct questions with direct answers. Has no patience for performance or status games. The size and the scales do most of the work and he knows it.

## 3 — MYSTIC: Liss Oru

**Species:** Memish (Bioluminescent markings, adapted for depth — dim markings shift with stress)  
**Heritage:** Mem  
**Background:** Faith  
**Vice:** Obligation (the Temple of Nur-thulama. She left but she can't pretend she's not still bound to something)

**The short version:** Way-touched from childhood. The Memish mystics at the Nur-thulama temple trained her — rigorously, unkindly. She left Mem after a schism she won't discuss in detail, arrived on Sonhandra on a freight manifest that didn't name her, and has been in the Warrens for eight months. She doesn't like the signal she feels in the Sump. Something there is listening and it isn't the Way she knows.

**Action Ratings**
- Attune 3, Study 2, Sway 1, Skulk 1

**Starting Special Ability:** The Way  
**Second Ability (choose one):** Strange Methods or Warded

**Default Load:** Light (3)  
**Notable items:** Precursor Artifact (a fragment from the Nur-thulama temple — warm to the touch, sometimes feels like it moves), Offerings, Fine Melee Weapon

**Contacts in Songah**
- *The Stitch* — Mira asks no questions about the bioluminescent markings. Liss pays in advance, comes when she needs to, leaves when she's done.
- *The Sump* — she's been mapping the signal variation down there. Someone or something in the Hollows is connected to what she felt on Mem. She hasn't approached them yet.

**Personality hook:** Speaks slowly and precisely. Never wastes words. Has the quality of always seeming like she arrived at a conclusion before you did. Not cold — she notices people — but she weighs every interaction against a set of considerations she doesn't share.

## 4 — PILOT: "Rook" Saren Vash

**Species:** Human  
**Heritage:** Holt  
**Background:** Trade  
**Vice:** Gambling (not money specifically — outcomes. He bets on things that can't be measured)

**The short version:** Rook ran cargo between Sonhandra and Trade Platform A for five years, co-owning his ship with a partner. The ship was seized by Hegemony Customs on a forged manifest claim eighteen months ago. His partner took a quiet settlement. Rook didn't. He's been doing shuttle runs and odd courier work since, saving toward a ship and trying to prove the seizure was engineered. He hasn't proven it yet. He suspects Cobalt Corporation. He could be right.

**Action Ratings**
- Helm 3, Rig 2, Scramble 2, Hack 1

**Starting Special Ability:** Ace Pilot  
**Second Ability (choose one):** Bravado or Ghost

**Default Load:** Normal (5)  
**Notable items:** Fine Customized Spacesuit, Grappling Hook, Communicator

**Contacts in Songah**
- *Central Terminal* — knows which inspectors run which shifts and which ones have flexible paperwork. Useful for moving cargo that shouldn't be moved.
- *Jerek's Junkyard* — has an open conversation with Jerek about a specific ship frame. Jerek is holding it. Rook doesn't have the cred yet.

**Personality hook:** Perpetually confident about physical problems, perpetually angry about political ones. Flies better under pressure than in calm conditions. Has a habit of narrating what he's doing while he does it — not nervousness, just how his brain works.

## 5 — SCOUNDREL: Nev

**Species:** Human  
**Heritage:** Akvi (frontier)  
**Background:** Criminal  
**Vice:** Luxury (not wealth specifically — access. Eating where they shouldn't be able to afford to eat. Wearing things that don't belong in the Warrens)  
**Pronouns:** they/them

**The short version:** Nev runs the Strip as an independent, which means not for any single faction but with debts to all of them. They broker information, run small cons, and occasionally steal things that need to be stolen. Currently owe Dovin Rek a significant amount from a deal that fell through — not Rek's money, technically, but Rek is the one who wants it back, which is the same problem. The amount is not ruinous. The principle is becoming one.

**Action Ratings**
- Skulk 3, Sway 2, Study 2, Scramble 1

**Starting Special Ability:** Serendipitous  
**Second Ability (choose one):** Ghost or Like Looking in a Mirror

**Default Load:** Light (3)  
**Notable items:** Fine Sidearm (pair), Forged Documents, Fine Coat, Loaded Dice / Trick Holocards

**Contacts in Songah**
- *Sable's Club* — Sable owes them a small favor from a piece of information Nev brought them two years ago. It's a small debt but a real one.
- *Dovin Rek's House of Fortune* — they owe Rek. Rek knows it. This is the current active complication in their life.

**Personality hook:** Charming in a way that feels like they're genuinely interested in you, which sometimes they are. Never commits to anything until they've mapped the exits. Has opinions about everyone they've met and will not share them unless asked directly, at which point the opinions are extremely specific.

## 6 — SPEAKER: Celene Adra

**Species:** Human  
**Heritage:** Holt  
**Background:** Political  
**Vice:** Obligation (she still believes in the idea of governance, even though she has no illusions about the practice of it)

**The short version:** Former administrative attaché to the Governor of Sonhandra, during the previous administration. When the Governor rotated out, the whole department went with him. She came down from the Upper Spires to Central City with savings, a network of people who still return her calls, and the accumulated institutional knowledge of how the Hegemony's Sonhandra apparatus actually works. She does not tell people what she used to be. She lets them find out later, when it matters.

**Action Ratings**
- Sway 3, Consort 2, Study 2, Command 1

**Starting Special Ability:** Air of Respectability  
**Second Ability (choose one):** Disarming or The Devil's Own Tongue

**Default Load:** Light (3)  
**Notable items:** Fine Clothes, Legitimate ID, Luxury Items (small), Memento of a Past Encounter (a sealed letter she has not opened)

**Contacts in Songah**
- *Civic Authority Post* — several former colleagues still in the city law enforcement wing. Not allies, but people who remember her name and what it was attached to.
- *The Canopy* — still has an access name from the old days. It hasn't been flagged yet.

**Personality hook:** Unhurried. Never raises her voice. When she stops talking in the middle of a sentence and waits, people rush to fill the silence in her favor. Knows more than she shows about almost every situation and has a precise sense of when to show it.

## 7 — STITCH: "Patch" Domal

**Species:** Chelkin (Alpine coloring — grey-white banding, compact build, characteristically steady six-limbed grip)  
**Heritage:** Sohan  
**Background:** Labor  
**Vice:** Obligation (the workers he couldn't save in the Sohan incident. He keeps a list)

**The short version:** Domal worked the Sohan Industrial District medical response teams for nine years — mining accidents, toxic exposures, explosive decompression, industrial burns. He was good at it. When a Guild quota increase caused a preventable mass casualty event in the deep mines and the Guild buried the inquiry, he quit, filed a formal complaint that went nowhere, and ended up in the Warrens. His skills are more valued here, even if the pay is worse. He collaborates with Mira at The Stitch on complex cases when the resources are available.

**Action Ratings**
- Doctor 3, Study 2, Sway 1, Rig 1

**Starting Special Ability:** I'm a Doctor, Not a...  
**Second Ability (choose one):** Steady Hands or All That I Need

**Default Load:** Normal (5)  
**Notable items:** Fine Medkit, Fine Bedside Manner (a practiced clinical calm that reads as competence and makes patients cooperate), Syringes and Applicators, Candies and Treats

**Contacts in Songah**
- *The Stitch* — Mira. The closest thing he has to a colleague. They share knowledge and sometimes patients, without a formal arrangement.
- *Labor Compact Hall* — he testified at the Compact's inquiry into the Sohan incident. They remember. He is welcome there.

**Personality hook:** Clinical in emergencies — quieter, steadier, faster than he is in normal conversation. Off-duty he's warm and a little scattered; on the job he's a different person. Has opinions about the Guild that he expresses concisely and then drops, because what else is there to say.

| 3 | Mystic | Memish | Way-manipulation / weird | The Sump signal, temple fragment |

Every character has a built-in personal thread that can be developed across sessions. None require a ship — the party starts ground-based in Songah. The Pilot's ship-recovery arc is the natural on-ramp to acquiring crew transport mid-campaign.
