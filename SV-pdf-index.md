### Load & Gear summary (p. 4 + p. 12)
- Characters have access to ALL items on their playbook sheet
- At mission start, choose load: **Light** (3), **Normal** (5), **Heavy** (6)
- Check off which items you're carrying — no purchasing, no shopping phase
- Unusual/temporary assets: use `acquire asset` downtime action (see p. 43)
